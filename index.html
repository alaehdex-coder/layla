(01) About

I want to teach English because words gave me a voice, and every student deserves one too.

Aila Mahinay is a second-year college student taking up Bachelor of Secondary Education, Major in English. She grew up reading out loud to anyone who would listen. Now she is studying grammar, literature and the art of teaching, so that one day she can help young people read closely, write clearly and speak with confidence.
